## PROCEDURE
1. Straightforward Prompting
Prompt:

"Explain the importance of artificial intelligence in healthcare in simple English. List three applications of AI in the healthcare industry."

Generated Output:

Artificial Intelligence (AI) plays an important role in healthcare by helping doctors analyze medical information and improve patient care.

Three applications of AI in healthcare are:
1. Disease Detection – AI helps identify diseases by analyzing medical images and patient information.
2. Medical Chatbots – AI chatbots provide basic health information and answer common medical questions.
3. Patient Monitoring – AI helps monitor patients' health conditions using smart devices.
Evaluation: The prompt generates a clear, simple, and relevant response.

## 2. Tabular Format Prompting
Prompt:

"Create a table showing five applications of AI in healthcare, their functions, and their benefits."

Generated Output:
Application	Function	Benefit
Disease Detection	Identifies diseases	Early detection
Medical Chatbots	Answers health questions	24/7 assistance
Medical Imaging	Analyzes X-rays and scans	Faster analysis
Patient Monitoring	Tracks health conditions	Continuous monitoring
Drug Discovery	Analyzes potential medicines	Faster research

Evaluation: The prompt successfully organizes information into a structured table, making comparison easier.

## 3. Preceding Question Prompting
Prompt:

"What are the common symptoms of diabetes? How can artificial intelligence help identify potential diabetes risks using patient information? Explain the role of AI in early disease detection."

Generated Output:

Common symptoms of diabetes include:
- Increased thirst.
- Frequent urination.
- Unusual tiredness.
- Increased hunger.
- Blurred vision.
AI can help identify potential diabetes risks by analyzing patient information such as age, blood glucose levels, weight, and medical history.

AI systems can identify patterns in the collected data and highlight individuals who may need further medical evaluation.

Evaluation: The prompt generates a detailed response by asking related questions in a logical sequence.
4. Missing Word Prompting
Prompt:

"Complete the following sentences related to AI-based healthcare:
1. Artificial Intelligence is commonly abbreviated as ______.
2. AI helps doctors detect diseases at an ______ stage.
3. Medical chatbots provide basic health ______.
4. AI can analyze medical images such as X-rays and ______."
Generated Output:
1. Artificial Intelligence is commonly abbreviated as AI.
2. AI helps doctors detect diseases at an early stage.
3. Medical chatbots provide basic health information.
4. AI can analyze medical images such as X-rays and MRI scans.
Evaluation: The prompt successfully fills the missing words with contextually appropriate answers.
