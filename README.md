## Explanation:

Prompt engineering is the process of designing and structuring instructions given to Artificial Intelligence (AI) models to generate meaningful, accurate, and relevant responses. A well-designed prompt helps an AI system understand the user's requirements and produce the expected output.

Different prompting techniques are used to control the response format, improve accuracy, provide context, and complete missing information.

In this experiment, four different prompting techniques are implemented and evaluated using the Smart Agriculture System.

### Types of Prompt Engineering

1. **Straightforward Prompting:** Giving a direct instruction to the AI model.
2. **Tabular Format Prompting:** Asking the AI model to organize information in rows and columns.
3. **Preceding Question Prompting:** Providing related questions before the main instruction to establish context.
4. **Missing Word Prompting:** Providing incomplete sentences with blanks for the AI model to complete.

## USE CASE
**AI-Based Healthcare Diagnosis System**

The objective is to use AI to provide basic health information based on symptoms entered by users. The system generates possible explanations and suggests whether medical consultation may be required.

## PROCEDURE

### 1. Straightforward Prompting

**Prompt:**

"Explain the importance of artificial intelligence in healthcare in simple English. List three applications of AI in the healthcare industry."

**Generated Output:**

Artificial Intelligence (AI) plays an important role in healthcare by helping doctors analyze medical information and improve patient care.

| S.No | Application | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Disease Detection | AI helps identify diseases by analyzing medical images and patient information. |
| 2 | Medical Chatbots | AI chatbots provide basic health information and answer common medical questions. |
| 3 | Patient Monitoring | AI helps monitor patients' health conditions using smart devices. |

**Evaluation:** The prompt generates a clear, simple, and relevant response.

### 2. Tabular Format Prompting

**Prompt:**

"Create a table showing five applications of AI in healthcare, their functions, and their benefits."

**Generated Output:**

| S.No | Application | Function | Benefit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Disease Detection | Identifies diseases | Early detection |
| 2 | Medical Chatbots | Answers health questions | 24/7 assistance |
| 3 | Medical Imaging | Analyzes X-rays and scans | Faster analysis |
| 4 | Patient Monitoring | Tracks health conditions | Continuous monitoring |
| 5 | Drug Discovery | Analyzes potential medicines | Faster research |

**Evaluation:** The prompt successfully organizes information into a structured table, making comparison easier.

### 3. Preceding Question Prompting

**Prompt:**

"What are the common symptoms of diabetes? How can artificial intelligence help identify potential diabetes risks using patient information? Explain the role of AI in early disease detection."

**Generated Output:**

| S.No | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What are the common symptoms of diabetes? | Increased thirst, frequent urination, unusual tiredness, increased hunger, and blurred vision. |
| 2 | How can AI identify potential diabetes risks? | AI analyzes patient information such as age, blood glucose levels, weight, and medical history. |
| 3 | What is the role of AI in early disease detection? | AI identifies patterns in collected data and highlights individuals who may need further medical evaluation. |

**Evaluation:** The prompt generates a detailed response by asking related questions in a logical sequence.

### 4. Missing Word Prompting

**Prompt:**

1. Artificial Intelligence is commonly abbreviated as ______.

3. Medical chatbots provide basic health ______.

**Generated Output:**

| S.No | Sentence | Missing Word |

| 2 | AI helps doctors detect diseases at an ______ stage. | Early |
| 3 | Medical chatbots provide basic health ______. | Information |

**Evaluation:** The prompt successfully fills the missing words with contextually appropriate answers.
